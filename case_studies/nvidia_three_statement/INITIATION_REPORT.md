# NVIDIA Initiation-Style Equity Research Report

**Website section:** **07 | NVIDIA Yahoo Finance Research** -> **Download NVIDIA research report (PDF)**.

## Deliverable
A date-stamped professional PDF that includes:
1. Research thesis and investment-view classification (educational **Not Rated**).
2. Company and industry overview.
3. Dated second-quarter FY2027 reported financial facts, explicitly differentiated from hypothetical projections.
4. Latest available Yahoo annual financials and market close, separately labelled with reference dates.
5. Five-year FCFF forecasts, cash-to-equity bridge, 3 scenario valuations and WACC vs terminal-growth sensitivity.
6. Potential catalysts, downside risks, required diligence and primary-source links.

## Sources and dating
- [NVIDIA 26 August 2026 FY2027 Q2 earnings release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/), reporting Q2 revenue of US$96.221bn, Data Center revenue of US$89.0bn, and then-current Q3 revenue guidance of US$108bn +/- 2%.
- [NVIDIA reports and regulatory filings](https://investor.nvidia.com/financial-info/financial-reports/)
- [Yahoo Finance NVDA](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NVDA/), for live *dated* annual statements and share price.

The **qualitative research was reviewed 12 November 2026**. It is not automatically revised when NVIDIA publishes later 10-Q/10-K documents. Generated PDFs clearly distinguish their creation date from the research-review date and market-observation date. The DCF continues to anchor to annual (not intra-year) Yahoo statements and does not pretend to update quarterly business drivers.

## Limitations and integrity
Forecast growth, profitability and FCFF drivers are illustrative assumptions, **not** company guidance or vetted Street consensus. WACC and terminal growth materially affect terminal value, and Yahoo financial classifications and the diluted weighted-average share count require validation against filings. This is not a Buy/Sell recommendation, not licensed financial advice, and not validated institutional research.

The independent project uses AI-assisted software development. Financial concepts, source evidence and modelling assumptions still require critical human review. Test the offline PDF generator with `python -m pytest -q` (test_research_upgrades.py).
